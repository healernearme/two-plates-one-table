Two diets, one shopping basket

Gluten-free (you): stock cubes, mustard, oats, soy/tamari and even some spice blends can carry wheat as a filler or thickener — check the label each time, or look for a "gluten-free" mark.

Dairy-free except butter (him): feta, yogurt-based sauces, cream and hard cheese are swapped to a dairy-free alternative or simply left off his plate in the recipes — butter, ghee and olive oil are all fine for him.
Built for a ~1,300 kcal/day target with reduced activity in mind — if you're often hungry or your energy dips, add extra vegetables, a tablespoon more olive oil, or a small piece of fruit rather than skipping meals. Calorie figures are estimates from standard ingredient values. Ocado links open a product search on ocado.com — there's no free "add to basket" API available to personal projects, so this is the closest one-click option.
